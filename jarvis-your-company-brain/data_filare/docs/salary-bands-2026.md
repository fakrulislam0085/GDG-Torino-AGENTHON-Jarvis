# Salary bands 2026 (confidential, finance only)

| Level | Gross yearly salary |
|---|---|
| Junior engineer | EUR 32,000 – 38,000 |
| Senior engineer | EUR 45,000 – 55,000 |
| Field engineer | EUR 36,000 – 44,000 |

The budget for the open senior backend hire is EUR 52,000 gross a year.
