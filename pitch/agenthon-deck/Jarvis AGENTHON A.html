Tap Approve, and only then it happens

Every morning, each person gets their own brief. A person approves what Jarvis remembers. Anyone can ask, by text or voice, and gets sources or "I don't know". When an urgent email lands, Jarvis alerts the CEO and drafts the fix. Nothing happens until a person taps Approve.
