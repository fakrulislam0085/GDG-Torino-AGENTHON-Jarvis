# Customer handbook

- Support hours: Monday to Friday, 08:00–18:00. During frost season (November to April) field support is also on call at weekends.
- Promise to customers: we answer a support email within 4 working hours and send an engineer within 2 working days for an outage.
- Every subscription renews yearly. A customer can pause a renewal with 30 days' notice.
